00 — Approach

Engineering is more than creating drawings. It’s about making ideas understandable, practical, and buildable.

I approach every drawing as a set of instructions someone will build from. That means accurate dimensions, clear annotation, and layouts that respect how systems are actually installed and maintained on site. Good documentation saves time, prevents mistakes, and keeps everyone — engineers, contractors, and owners — working from the same picture.

I keep learning with every project: better drafting standards, sharper coordination, and a deeper understanding of the mechanical systems behind each line.
